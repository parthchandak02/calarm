Background sync now actually runs — the 6am and hourly refreshes were registered too late to ever install. Meetings that arrive from both Google and iOS Calendar no longer produce two alarms. Focus blocks no longer ring. Holiday calendars can now be subscribed to. Alarms without a Live Activity now carry a one second pre-alert, a workaround for AlarmKit failing to present in landscape. This build also records intended versus actual fire times so late or missed alarms can be measured rather than guessed at.
