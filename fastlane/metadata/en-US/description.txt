CALarm turns your calendar events into real alarms. Tap an event and CALarm rings before it starts, with a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you never miss a meeting.

FEATURES
• Your upcoming week, grouped by day
• One or more alarms per event, from 1 minute to 1 day before
• A default alarm time and snooze length you choose
• Live countdowns on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Works with the calendars already on your iPhone
• Optional: connect Google Calendar for faster updates from Google
• Light, dark or system appearance, with accent colors
• Private by design: your calendar stays on your iPhone. No account, no servers, no tracking

CALarm requires iOS 26 or later.
