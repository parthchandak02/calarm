CALarm requires iOS 26 or later for AlarmKit.

No Calarm account is required. By default the app reads the reviewer’s on-device calendar (EventKit); nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Optional: Settings → Google Calendar → Sign in with Google to sync events from a Google account via the Google Calendar API. This is not required for review.

To test:
1. Grant Calendar access when prompted.
2. Grant Alarms permission when prompted (Settings → CALarm if denied).
3. Open the week schedule, tap an event, and add one or more alarm offsets.
4. Observe the countdown in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

There is no demo account. The app works with the reviewer’s on-device calendar without Google sign-in.
